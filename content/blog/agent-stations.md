A chatbot on a backlog is not a multi-agent system. We split the floor the way a factory splits stations.

> Agents move the tickets. People own the gates that cannot be faked.

That pattern is how we keep **Repdaily**, **ReadyGo**, and **Contentic** in production instead of in a demo reel.
