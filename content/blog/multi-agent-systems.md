AI workflow architecture is a core capability here, not a side experiment. Autonomous agents, orchestrated pipelines, and review gates where the work demands a human.

> Agents move the tickets. People own the gates that cannot be faked.

## What that looks like in production

We split work the way a factory splits stations: research, implementation, verification, and release. Agents move tickets through those stations. People own the gates that cannot be faked — product judgement, security, and the last mile into production.

That is the difference between a demo and a system you can run every week. The same pattern shows up across **Repdaily**, **ReadyGo**, and **Contentic**.
