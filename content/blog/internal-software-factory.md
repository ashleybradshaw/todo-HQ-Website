Most teams treat each engagement as a fresh start. We do not. The same pipeline that ships our own apps is the pipeline we run for technical founders and product leads.

> A factory is a pipeline you can run next week. Freelance is a hero you hope shows up.

## Repeatable on purpose

An internal software factory means the work has a shape: scoped intake, multi-agent implementation, human-in-the-loop gates, and a production handoff. Velocity without a disposable architecture.

### Intake before agents

Scoped intake is the first station. Without it, the floor is just a queue of vibes.

The roster is the proof. **Repdaily**, **ReadyGo**, and **Contentic** all leave the same floor.
