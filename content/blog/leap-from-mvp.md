We do not treat a first cut as a one-off. The leap is wiring it back into intake, agents, and release so the next week is still a factory.

> A leap is a pipeline you can run again, not a larger hero week.

That is how **Repdaily**, **ReadyGo**, and **Contentic** stayed on the roster instead of in a graveyard of MVPs.
